## Summary
> [!ABSTRACT] Summary
> Fr. Joseph Iannuzzi clarifies that while we are living in the "end times" prophesied by the Blessed Mother at Garabandal and Fatima, this is distinct from the end of the world. We are currently witnessing an intensification of both evil and good. The present era is a preparation for a "glorious era of peace," which will be preceded by a period of divine purification (purgation). He emphasizes that God's wrath is not vengeful but a remedial act of mercy and justice, intended to sanctify humanity.

## Main Points
### The Nuance of Prophecy
- The "end of time" signifies the end of the reign of the human will and the beginning of a sequence leading to a glorious era of peace (Revelation 20).

### Divine Wrath and Justice
- God's wrath is intimately united with His mercy; it is a purgative remedy comparable to a doctor applying pain to ensure recovery.

### The Three Future Events
- 1. A day of blazing wrath/chastisement that purifies the elements and humanity.
- 2. The emergence of a holy remnant/priesthood that glorifies God.
- 3. The interior, hidden reign of Christ (Era of Peace/Sabbath Rest) before His final visible coming.
